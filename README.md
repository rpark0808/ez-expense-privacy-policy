# Privacy Policy for EZ Expense

EZ Expense ("we", "our", or "the app") helps users track and manage personal expenses. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

Information We Process

EZ Expense may process the following information:

Expense Data: Transaction details, categories, tags, notes, dates, and locations you add to manage your expenses.
SMS Messages (Optional): If you enable automatic expense detection, the app reads bank transaction SMS messages to identify expense details. SMS data is processed only for this purpose and is not shared or uploaded.
Files: CSV files or backup files you choose to import or export.
Location (Optional): Used only when you choose to attach a location to an expense.
How We Use Information

Your information is used only to provide app features such as:

Expense tracking and organization
Automatic transaction detection
Reports and charts
Data import, export, and backup features

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers.

Data Storage

Most expense data is stored locally on your device. Some optional features may use secure third-party services to provide functionality. Data is handled according to applicable privacy and security practices.

Permissions

EZ Expense may request permissions for:

SMS access for automatic expense detection
File access for importing/exporting data
Background processing for supported features

You can manage or revoke permissions from your Android device settings.

Data Deletion

You can delete your expense data within the app. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored data from your device.
